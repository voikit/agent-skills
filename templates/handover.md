# Handover document - in the owner's language

Build it from the account as it is now (`account_overview`, `callcenter_get_config`, `agents_list`, `memory_get`, `knowledge_list`, `list_changes`), not from memory of what was planned.

## 1. What we built
A list: each item in one line with the panel page where the owner finds it. Operators, call routing, knowledge, charter rules, CRM follow-ups, channels, booking.

## 2. Why it is built this way
The three decisions that shaped the setup (answering mode, what the operator may not promise, when it hands over) and the reason for each, in the owner's own terms.

## 3. How to change things yourself
Five everyday jobs. For each: the panel page, or the sentence to say to Voikit's built-in assistant or to your own connected assistant.
- change business hours or add a holiday
- change the greeting
- add a question and its answer
- add or remove a colleague who receives calls
- see what an assistant changed and undo it

## 4. Test results
The go-live checklist with pass / fail per line, the score, and anything still open with its owner and date.

## 5. The next 30 days
What we review and when (daily in week one, a week-one report, a day-30 review), and the two things we need from you: tell us when the operator says something wrong, and approve or reject the suggestions that appear in the panel.

## 6. Who to contact
The deployment lead and how to reach them; how to open a support ticket.

## 7. Your approved red lines
The hard rules, word for word, as a reminder of what was agreed.
