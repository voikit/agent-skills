# Setup proposal - one page, in the owner's language

Write it in plain words for a business owner, not for a technician. No product jargon: say "phone menu", not IVR; "who answers", not routing. Every line the owner did not give you is marked "(default for your kind of business)". Nothing is built until the owner approves this page.

## 1. This is how we understood you
Three to five lines. Each ends with its source: (your words) / (your website) / (your profile in Voikit).

## 2. This is how your phone will work
- During business hours: ...
- After hours and on holidays: ...
- When a colleague does not answer: ...
One sentence naming the answering mode and why it fits.

## 3. Your AI operators
For each: name - what it does in one sentence - the greeting, word for word.

## 4. The operator will never ... / will always connect to a person when ...
The hard rules and the hand-over cases, as the owner said them.

## 5. What it knows
The knowledge sources: website, Q&A, products and prices, documents the owner will upload.

## 6. Follow-ups
Which kinds of work are tracked in the CRM, which operator follows each up, how many attempts and how far apart.

## 7. Channels and booking
What is switched on now, what waits for the owner to connect in the panel.

## 8. Defaults we chose for your kind of business
A short list. Each can be changed with one sentence.

## 9. What we need from you
Panel-only items (verifying colleagues' mobiles, the phone number, files to upload) with who does them and by when.

## Approval
"I approve" / "Change this line: ____"
